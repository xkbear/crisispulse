Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2026
TL;DR

We don't collect your personal data. We don't use cookies, third-party trackers, or analytics scripts. Your location stays on your device. Your email — only if you choose to subscribe — is used solely to send you conflict alerts. That's it.

1. Who we are

Crisis Pulse (https://crisispulse.org) and the Crisis Pulse iOS app ("the Service") are operated by an independent developer as a free, open-source project. The Service is not affiliated with any government, NGO, media organization, or commercial preparedness vendor.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not use cookies on the website.
  • We do not embed third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.).
  • We do not track you across other sites or apps.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with advertisers.
  • We do not require you to create an account.

3. Information we do handle

3.1 Anonymous visitor counts (website)

The website displays a simple visitor counter grouped by country. Country is inferred from the request's network metadata (Netlify's built-in geolocation header) on the server side. We do not store IP addresses. We store only a per-country tally (e.g. "United Kingdom: 312"). No identifier links any visit back to an individual user.

3.2 Location (iOS app, optional)

If you grant the iOS app "When In Use" location permission, your latitude/longitude is used only on your device to:

  • Position the green "you are here" dot on the map.
  • Compute a relative risk index for your area.

Your coordinates are never transmitted to our servers and never stored anywhere. You can revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Crisis Pulse.

3.3 Email address (only if you subscribe)

If — and only if — you choose to subscribe to the email brief, we store your email address along with the country reported by your network metadata at signup time. We use this address solely to send you:

  • A one-time welcome confirmation email.
  • The daily Top-5 conflicts brief.
  • Escalation alerts when a tracked conflict's intensity score rises significantly within 24 hours.

We use Resend as the email delivery provider. Resend processes your email solely to deliver the message and is bound by their own privacy policy. We never share your email with anyone else.

You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing hello@crisispulse.org with the subject "unsubscribe", and your address will be removed within 7 days.

3.4 Local notifications (iOS app, optional)

If you enable in-app notifications, the iOS app schedules alerts locally on your device using Apple's UserNotifications framework. We do not run a push server and do not have access to your APNs token. No notification content leaves your device.

4. News content

The conflict intensity scores and headlines you see on the map are derived from public news feeds (Bing News RSS) by an automated daily job. We do not collect or store user interaction data with the news content. Outbound links open the publisher's site directly — that publisher's own privacy policy applies once you leave Crisis Pulse.

5. Children

The Service is rated 12+ in the App Store due to news/political content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has subscribed to the email brief, please email us and we will remove the address.

6. Data security

The website is served over HTTPS with HSTS preload. Subscriber emails are stored in Netlify Blobs (a managed key-value store) accessible only to the Service's serverless functions. The dataset contains only email + country code + signup timestamp — no passwords, no payment information, no other personal details.

7. International data transfer

Crisis Pulse is hosted on Netlify's global CDN, which may store and process data in multiple regions. The email service provider (Resend) operates from the United States. By using the Service, you consent to your email (if you subscribe) being processed in the US.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and similar regulations, you have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we hold your email address.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you (email + country + signup date is the entire record).
  • Request deletion of your record.
  • Withdraw consent for the email brief at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@crisispulse.org. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Apple App Store privacy nutrition label

The Crisis Pulse iOS app reports the following data types to the App Store Connect privacy questionnaire:

  • Location (Coarse / Precise) — Optional. Used only on the device to position your map marker. Not linked to identity. Not used for tracking.
  • Email Address — Optional. Collected only if you subscribe to the brief. Used for service-related communications. Not linked to identity for advertising. Not used for tracking.

No other data types are collected.

10. Open source & transparency

The full source code of both the website and the iOS app is public. You can inspect every line of how the Service handles data:

  • Repository: github.com/xkbear/crisispulse

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date at the top will change and we will post a notice on the homepage for at least 30 days. We will not retroactively use previously collected data in ways inconsistent with the policy in effect at the time of collection.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@crisispulse.org.
