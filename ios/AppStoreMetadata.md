```
Crisis Pulse is a free, interactive crisis map that tracks 25+ active global conflicts — Russia–Ukraine, Gaza, Sudan, Myanmar, Taiwan Strait, Yemen, Haiti, Iran, and more — with daily intensity scoring drawn from real news feeds. Pair it with a personalized emergency-supply calculator tailored to your country, home type, and household size, and you have everything you need to turn awareness into a concrete preparedness plan.

No signup. No tracking. No paywall. Just an honest tool.

WHY CRISIS PULSE
• A real-time crisis map, not a doom feed. The map shows where instability is concentrated and how each situation is trending today.
• Daily intensity scoring. An automated pipeline reads global news volume per conflict and computes a 0–10 score updated every 24 hours.
• NEW badges. Newly emerged or news-spiking conflicts are flagged so you spot escalations the moment they start trending.
• Today's Top Story. The single most-reported event of the day floats to the top with BREAKING / ESCALATING / HIGH ATTENTION badges.
• Tailored prep checklists. Tell us your country, housing type, household size, and special needs (infants, pets, medical) — get a tiered list (0–72 hours / 1 week+ / 1 month+) with quantities you can actually act on.

WHO USES IT
• International travelers and expatriates needing situational awareness across regions
• Journalists, researchers, and OSINT analysts
• Policy observers and students of geopolitics
• Households in affected regions wanting practical guidance
• Anyone who prefers signal over panic

PRIVACY FIRST
• No signup. No accounts. No password to forget.
• Your location stays on your device. Used only to position your dot on the map.
• No third-party analytics. No advertisers.
• Optional email brief — opt in any time, opt out any time.

NATIVE iOS FEATURES
• MapKit world map with smooth gestures
• Local push alerts when a tracked conflict's intensity rises significantly
• Bilingual English / 简体中文 — switch at any time
• Share sheet integration for conflicts and your supply plan
• Dark mode by default

OPEN SOURCE
The entire project is public on GitHub. You can read every line of how it works.

Crisis Pulse is built around one core idea: awareness before action. Most people only think about preparedness when something is already escalating near them — by then, supply chains are stressed and the calm decision-making window has closed. Crisis Pulse shortens the distance between noticing a global event and doing something concrete about it at the household level.

For a quieter, lower-anxiety relationship with global news, give it a try.

Web: crisispulse.org
Source: github.com/xkbear/crisispulse
```

## Privacy Nutrition Label (Apple App Store Connect)

When prompted "Does your app collect data?", choose **Yes** and report:

### Data Linked to User → Email Address
- **Used for**: App Functionality
- **Linked to identity**: NO (we use it only to send the brief, not to identify you across services)
- **Used for tracking**: NO

### Data Not Linked to User → Coarse Location, Precise Location
- **Used for**: App Functionality
- **Used for tracking**: NO
- *Note: location is processed only on-device and never transmitted — but Apple still requires us to declare it because we ask for the permission.*

### All other categories
Select **NO** for: Health, Financial, Contacts, User Content, Browsing History, Search History, Identifiers, Purchases, Usage Data, Diagnostics, Sensitive Info, Other Data.

## Build configuration before archive

In Xcode, before **Product → Archive**:

1. Pick the **Any iOS Device (arm64)** destination (not a simulator)
2. Select **Release** scheme
3. Confirm `MARKETING_VERSION = 0.2.0` and `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION = 1` in `project.yml`
4. Run **Product → Archive**
5. Window → Organizer → **Distribute App → App Store Connect → Upload**
6. Wait ~10 min for processing in App Store Connect, then attach the build to your draft listing.

Submit for review with the metadata above. Typical approval window: 24–48 hours.
